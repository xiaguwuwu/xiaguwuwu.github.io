Free-Queue Wait Estimates

Product
VerseIn
Role
Product Manager
Dates
2026.09 – 2026.10

Project

Subscribers can generate videos for free but have to queue. Releases are throttled by a daily budget and light users go first, so the queue is not first come, first served.

The first two versions both changed how the wait was displayed:

Process

Fill in the data

The old dashboard only counted jobs that were released, so people who gave up were not in the denominator. First added tracking: promised time, actual release time, and when users left the queue and where they went.

Find the cause

Compared promise with reality across a week of 692 release records. The problem was not the display; the first estimate was already too optimistic:

Look around

Studied 7 products. Those with minute-level waits show no time at all, those that show a queue position all ran into trouble, and if you do show a number, too long is safer than too short.

Design

Results

  • Better display only cuts complaints; shorter waits need a bigger budget or moving expensive models out of the free channel. Both were raised with the business side at the same time.
