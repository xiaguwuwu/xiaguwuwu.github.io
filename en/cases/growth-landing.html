Ad Landing Pages and New-User Conversion

Product
VerseIn
Role
Product Manager
Dates
2026.10 – 2026.11

Project

  • The ads team brought a batch of long-tail search terms, but new landing pages could only be added in code by developers, so every new term needed a dev slot and a release.
  • Source attribution got lost, and the path from visitor to paying user broke in several places.

Process

Landing pages

PageTraffic it catchesHow it’s configured
Template theme pageSearch terms for a type of effectOps enters a term in the backend and the page is generated
Model pageSearch terms for a specific modelCopy, FAQ and examples configured per model
Long-tail keyword workbenchCompetitor names, curiosity termsGenerate right on the landing page, routed by balance
Scenario workbenchSpecific industry scenariosEach scenario is a config; new scenarios need no release

New-user conversion

Walked the 5 stages and 20 steps from landing to payment and marked what was hard-coded and what was configurable. Drawing on competitors and e-commerce, listed 33 conversion hooks and ranked 13 for phase one by effort and impact. Three key decisions:

  • The first video must be fully payable with the sign-up bonus coins.
  • No fake “generating” animation; show a real prompt instead, and submit automatically after sign-in.
  • Mechanics are fixed in code; copy, frequency and price live in the backend.

Rule conflicts found

ConflictFix
The first-month discount required “never paid”, so buying a coin pack removed it for goodChanged to “never subscribed”
Email sign-up dropped landing parameters, losing about 75% of source attributionRecord the first landing page and source; send only real payments back to ad platforms
The new-user pack sold coins, but free users couldn’t buy coin packsDropped the new-user pack; everything goes through subscriptions

Results

  • The first theme pages and model pages are live: ops builds them in the backend with no dev slot or release, and FAQs on the first 18 model pages cover 8 languages. The other two page types are still in requirements.
  • Found that email sign-up loses about 75% of ad attribution; the fix is in phase one.
  • The first-month discount and anti-abuse rules for sign-up coins are live; audience banners on the pricing page and audience filters for promotions are in development.
